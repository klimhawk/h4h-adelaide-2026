Hack for Humanity Adelaide

A free, one-afternoon build. Join a team and build something with Gemma, Google's open AI model, that could help someone in South Australia. You do not need to code.

  • When: Friday 30 October 2026, 3 to 8pm.
  • Where: AI Masters Dojo, Level 3, 97 Pirie St, Adelaide.
  • Themes: health, education, sustainability.
  • Hosted by AI Collective Adelaide, as a Hacktoberfest Hack Day with Major League Hacking (MLH).
