Data

Real open data about South Australia, already downloaded and checked, so your team spends the afternoon building, not searching. Any track can use it; track 2 is built on it.

Get it: our GitHub repo has the full guide (what each dataset tells you, its source and licence) and the files.

What is in it

ThemeFor example
HealthPeople using aged care, by region; deaths and their causes, country SA against Adelaide
EducationOccupations in shortage in SA; online job ads by occupation and region
SustainabilityRooftop solar and batteries by postcode; how full Adelaide's reservoirs are each month
Every themeCensus profiles, population, income, advantage and disadvantage (SEIFA), map boundaries

Tips

  • Start small: one dataset you can understand in 15 minutes, then add context.
  • Join on a shared key, such as SA2 area, council area or postcode. Counts become "per person in this suburb". The repo guide lists which datasets share which key.
  • Credit the source: each dataset's folder has its licence and credit line. Put the credit lines in your README and on your final slide. Four sets on older licences (H2, F6, F8, S10) also need the licence text, or a link to it, with any copy you share.

Track 3 adds data from the Snowflake Marketplace, under its own terms: see Build and the Snowflake how-to.
