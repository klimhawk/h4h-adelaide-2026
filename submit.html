Submit and win

The challenges

One project can enter all three.

Best Use of Gemma 4

You qualify if: your app uses a Gemma model through the Gemini API, your README names the model, and you show it working.

Best Open-Source AI Project

You qualify if: open-source or open-weight AI (Gemma counts) is an important part of your project; your code is in a public GitHub repo with an open-source licence; your README names the model and links its licence. You do not need DigitalOcean.

Best Open-Source AI Project with Snowflake

You qualify if: you meet the open-source conditions, plus a free Snowflake Marketplace dataset that you name, and you show how CoCo helped.

How to meet them: Build, with the GitHub, Gemma and Snowflake how-tos.

How you are judged

Judges watch your demo and score each of these from 1 to 5:

  • Technical implementation: it works, and the AI does real work in it.
  • Creativity: a fresh idea or a clever build, easy to use.
  • Learning and ambition: what you tried that was new to you. Say it in the demo.
  • Demo and clarity: show it working; explain the problem, what you built and how it works.
  • Humanity: a real Adelaide or SA problem that could help someone today. It also picks a Hack for Humanity special mention.

Not scored: code quality or a sales pitch. Judges score what they see working. Before the demos we check the conditions above; a project that misses one cannot win that challenge.

The demo: three minutes from one laptop, then two minutes of questions. Record a 60-second screen capture of your working app beforehand, in case the live demo fails.

How to submit

  1. Check in at the door when you arrive. Only checked-in people can submit.
  2. One person submits for the team, signed in with the account they registered with.
  3. On the MLH event page, open Challenges, then Submit a Project: your repo link, the project name, a description, the technologies (include your AI coding tools), and tick every challenge you qualify for.
  4. Submit before submissions close: see the schedule.

After the event

Keep your repo public. MLH can choose new winners if it is not.
