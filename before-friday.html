Before Friday

You can bring an idea, but all project work happens on the day (MLH rule). Before Friday, set up accounts and tools only.

Your role

Teams are four to five people. Not everyone codes. Pick a role, or two:

  • Builder (one or two per team): builds the app with an AI coding tool.
  • Data: finds and checks the data.
  • Research and testing: checks the idea holds up, and tests the app and Gemma's answers.
  • Demo lead: plans the three-minute demo and submits the project.

Everyone does the first list below. Builders also do the second; for track 3, whoever works on the data also does the third.
